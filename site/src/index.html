Questions

Do I need to know how to code?+

No. You edit one text file on github.com and press Commit. The docs walk you through each step.

Can I use my own domain?+

Yes. Add your domain in your repository's Pages settings and point your DNS at GitHub. The docs show how.

Can I switch themes later?+

Any time. Change the theme line in profile.hjson and commit. Your links stay exactly as they are.

What does it cost?+

Nothing. linkprofile is open source under the MIT licence, and GitHub Pages hosting is free for public repositories.
